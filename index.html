            How can we help?

            Send us a message and the FEROCIA team will reply by email, usually within one business day.

            Quick answers
            I didn't get my sign-in link

            Check spam or promotions, and wait a minute before asking for a new one. You can also type the 6-digit code from the same email.

            My ladder or results don't show

            Your account may still be in review. If it isn't, send us a message and tell us which ladder you played.

            How do I change my name or date of birth?

            Choose “My details” below and tell us what to change. They connect you to your history, so our team updates them for you.
